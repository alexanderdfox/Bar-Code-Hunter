# Jack's Pumpkin

A bright orange, Halloween-ready mixed drink that uses real pumpkin puree for body and flavor. It’s lightly spiced, citrusy, and festive without being overly sweet.

### Ingredients (1 serving)
- 1½ oz vodka (or tequila for a margarita-style version)
- 1½ oz fresh orange juice
- ½ oz pumpkin puree (plain, not pumpkin pie filling)
- ½ oz maple syrup (or simple syrup)
- ¼–½ oz fresh lemon or lime juice (for brightness)
- Optional: tiny pinch of pumpkin pie spice or cinnamon
- Ice

**Garnish ideas (Halloween flair):**
- Orange wheel or twist
- Cinnamon stick
- Black licorice stick or a candy pumpkin
- Rim the glass with orange sugar or a mix of sugar + pumpkin pie spice

### Instructions
1. Add the vodka (or tequila), orange juice, pumpkin puree, maple syrup, citrus juice, and spice (if using) to a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill with ice and shake hard for 15–20 seconds until well chilled and the pumpkin is fully incorporated.
3. Double-strain (to catch any puree bits) into a rocks glass or coupe over fresh ice, or serve up if preferred.
4. Garnish as desired.

The pumpkin puree gives it a natural orange color and creamy texture while the orange juice keeps it bright and seasonal. It tastes like a spiced pumpkin-orange creamsicle with a kick—perfect for a Halloween party.

**Batch tip:** Multiply the ingredients and mix in a pitcher (shake individual servings or stir well before pouring). Serve in a hollowed mini pumpkin for extra spooky points if you want to go all out. Enjoy responsibly!
