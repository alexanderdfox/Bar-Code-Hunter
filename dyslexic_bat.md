# Dyslexic Bat

A dark, slightly chaotic Halloween cocktail that looks like it flew in sideways.

## Ingredients (makes 1)

- 2 oz dark rum (or black vodka if you want extra bat-black)
- ¾ oz crème de cassis (or blackberry liqueur)
- ½ oz fresh lime juice
- ¼ oz simple syrup (or agave)
- 2–3 dashes orange bitters
- Splash of club soda or ginger beer
- Optional: tiny pinch of activated charcoal powder (food-grade) for deeper black color — stir well
- Garnish: 2–3 blackberries on a pick + a thin lime twist (or a plastic bat if you’re fancy)

## Method

1. Add rum, cassis, lime, simple syrup, bitters (and charcoal if using) to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake hard until well chilled.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over a big ice cube (or into a coupe if you prefer it up).
4. Top with a short splash of soda or ginger beer.
5. Garnish with the blackberries and lime twist.

## Why it’s a Dyslexic Bat

It’s dark, a little tart, a little sweet, and the flavors arrive slightly out of order — the berry hits first, the lime second, the rum last. Just like a bat that got the letters mixed up.

**Optional spooky upgrade**: rim the glass with black sanding sugar mixed with a little citric acid for a sour-bat edge.

Drink responsibly. Happy Halloween.
